02 / Selected history

Milestones on the journey.

A short timeline of widely documented business milestones. We can expand this section with sourced dates, achievements, and additional images as the project develops.

1995

Zip2 begins

Musk co-founded Zip2, an early internet software company focused on city guides and business directories.

1999

Online finance

After the sale of Zip2, he co-founded X.com, an online financial services company.

2002

SpaceX founded

SpaceX was established with the goal of lowering the cost of space transportation.

2000s

Electric vehicle era

Musk became closely associated with Tesla's growth and the broader development of electric mobility.

Timeline is intentionally brief and will need further sourcing and review before publication as a comprehensive biography.
